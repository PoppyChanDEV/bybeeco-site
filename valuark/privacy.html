Early-access privacy notice

For the Valuark interest and launch-update form · November 2026

What we collect

Our early-access form asks for your email address and consent to receive updates. It may also ask optional questions about your role and how many businesses you expect to evaluate.

Why we use it

We use responses to assess demand, understand potential users, and send product development or launch updates to people who opt in. Joining does not create a paid account.

Where it is processed

Signups are collected through Microsoft Forms, which operates under Microsoft's applicable terms and privacy practices. Clicking the signup button takes you to Microsoft's form page.

Website analytics

We use Cloudflare Web Analytics to understand visits and page activity on this website. Analytics data is processed by Cloudflare to help us evaluate interest in BybeeCo and Valuark. We do not use this analytics service to collect investment documents or bank credentials.

Your choices

You can request that we stop emailing you or delete the contact information we've collected by writing to chandler@bybeeco.net. Marketing emails should include a way to unsubscribe.

What we don't request

We do not ask for bank credentials, brokerage logins, Social Security numbers, or confidential investment documents through this early-access form.

Comparison tester opt-in

If you volunteer to test Valuark’s future comparison, the separate Microsoft Forms opt-in asks for your email and confirmation that you’re actively evaluating an acquisition and willing to try comparing your own deals when the tool is available. Do not submit information about your deals.

We use your email to identify duplicate test signups and count distinct commitments. Volunteering does not sign you up for marketing updates, require a call, create an account, or commit you to buy anything. To request deletion, contact chandler@bybeeco.net. We also look at aggregate visits to the Valuark and tester pages; we do not link those visits to submitted email addresses.

Contact

BybeeCo LLC · chandler@bybeeco.net

This notice describes the early-access and comparison tester forms, not a future production application. It should be updated before launching new features, tracking technologies, or data collection.
